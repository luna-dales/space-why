
# Space Why?

[View Luna’s engineering portfolio](https://luna-dales.github.io/space-why/)

The portfolio website was developed with help from AI for writing, code, and layout. It documents completed work, ongoing development, and project contributions.

Space Why? is an aerospace engineering project exploring high-power rocketry, flight computers, flight-data acquisition, thrust-vector control, simulation, testing, and accessible presentation of aerospace data.

## Project Areas

### Level 1 High Power Rocket Certification

Design, simulation, construction, launch preparation, flight, recovery, and evaluation of a high-power rocket for Level 1 certification.

### Flying Blind

Development of a custom rocket flight computer for sensor acquisition, onboard data logging, flight-state detection, recovery-system control, and future thrust-vector-control applications.

### Thrust Vector Control

Design and testing of a two-axis thrust-vector-control system, including mechanical gimbal development, a static-fire test stand, servo control, attitude estimation, and closed-loop control.

### Simulation and Flight Analysis

Use of tools including OpenRocket, RocketSim, MATLAB, and Simulink to model rocket performance and compare predicted behavior with experimental and flight data.

### Accessible Aerospace Data

Exploration of ways aerospace telemetry and engineering data can be communicated more effectively to people with low vision and other accessibility needs.
