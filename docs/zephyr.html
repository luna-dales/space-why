L1 Zephyr

I built and flew the Zephyr to earn my Tripoli Rocketry Association (TRA) Junior High Power Rocketry (HPR) Level 1 (L1) certification.

NAR and Tripoli exams passed · Rocket built · Simulations completed · Certification flight completed · Tripoli certification earned

Building a Zephyr rocket

I first passed the National Association of Rocketry (NAR) Junior Level 1 exam and built a Zephyr rocket for my certification flight. We attended two club launch events, but the weather prevented me from completing the flight.

Building the Zephyr gave me experience using epoxy to build a larger rocket with stronger assemblies.

I later took and passed the Tripoli Rocketry Association (TRA) Junior HPR exam. This time, the weather cooperated: I flew the Zephyr with Tripoli and earned my Junior HPR Level 1 certification. Read about the Tripoli Junior HPR Level 1 program.
