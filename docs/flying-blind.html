Green Eggs: the Flying Blind V3 test bed

I chose the Estes Green Eggs rocket as the test bed for Flying Blind V3 because its payload compartment provided an affordable way to carry and test the flight computer.

I started by learning to connect sensors, read them in software, and write data to a microSD card. Later versions changed both the electronics and the way I built them.

Learning to solder with low vision

Soldering was difficult because my vision makes small components and joints hard to see. My dad taught me the technique, and better soldering equipment, magnification, and practice helped me improve.

Hardware progression

From breadboard to circuit board

V1

Arduino Uno

Solderless breadboard, barometer and accelerometer breakout boards, microSD storage, and basic Arduino C++ software.

Learning to connect and communicate with components.

V2

Teensy 4.0

Soldered breadboard, breakout boards on headers, upgraded components, and improved software.

Moving from temporary connections to soldered assembly.

V3

Custom PCB

Teensy 4.0, a schematic and PCB laid out in Autodesk Fusion, soldered breakout boards, battery power, and an on/off switch.

Learning circuit layout and mechanical integration.

Next

TVC electronics

Teensy 4.1, upgraded components, servo connections, and lights and buttons for feedback and input.

In development; software revisions are ongoing.

Making an avionics sled

For V3, I made a 3D-printed electronics-bay sled to hold the computer inside the Green Eggs test rocket. This connected the circuit-board work to a physical integration problem: fitting and mounting the computer inside the rocket.

Flight test / What happened

The rocket flew. The logger didn’t record.

I flew the Green Eggs rocket carrying Flying Blind V3. After powering on the computer, we needed to reset the software to prepare it for recording. We missed that step, and no flight data was collected.

For the next attempt, our revised preflight procedure will include the reset and a check that logging has started before launch.
