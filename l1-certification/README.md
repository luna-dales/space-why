
# Level 1 High Power Rocket Certification

This section documents the design, simulation, construction, preparation, flight, recovery, and evaluation of the high-power rocket built for Level 1 certification.

## Project Goals

- Design and construct a flight-ready high-power rocket.
- Understand the requirements for Level 1 High Power Rocket certification.
- Model and evaluate the rocket's stability and predicted flight performance.
- Prepare and safely conduct the certification flight.
- Successfully recover the rocket.
- Compare predicted performance with actual flight results.
- Document the complete engineering and certification process.

## Project Sections

### Design

Rocket configuration, component selection, motor selection, recovery-system design, and design decisions.

### Simulation

OpenRocket simulations, stability analysis, predicted altitude, velocity, acceleration, and other flight-performance predictions.

### Construction

Documentation and photographs of the rocket build process.

### Preflight

Final mass and center-of-gravity measurements, stability verification, recovery-system preparation, motor preparation, and launch checklist.

### Flight

Documentation of the certification flight, including launch conditions, observations, photographs, video, and available flight data.

### Certification

Certification results and related documentation.

### Post-Flight Analysis

Comparison of predicted and actual flight performance, inspection of the recovered rocket, lessons learned, and potential design improvements.
