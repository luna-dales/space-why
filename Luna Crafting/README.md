# Luna Crafting media collection

Original family-provided photos and videos for the crafting portfolio at `docs/crafting.html`.

The page covers baking (learning, independent practice, the adult Milk Bar class, and teaching), crocheting, and metal jewelry making. Specific dates, course details, and pattern authors are not inferred from the media. The family supplied the Milk Bar class history; its linked Birthday Cake product page is background, not a record of attendance.

All 41 original JPEG photos and four original MOV videos are retained without modification. Smaller website copies are in `docs/assets/crafting/`: oriented JPEG photos with embedded metadata removed, H.264/AAC MP4 videos, and video posters. Videos retain their audio. The original screen-capture photo retains its visible screenshot controls.

`docs/assets/crafting/manifest.json` maps each original filename to its website copy, topic, description, and original SHA-256 checksum. The website presents all photos, including alternate views under expandable galleries, and all four videos. The baking progression is thematic; the photo order does not claim exact dates.

The jewelry page shows Luna’s wire arm piece and group bracelet/workshop photos. It does not attribute every bracelet in a group photo to her. Crochet pattern authorship is not claimed.
